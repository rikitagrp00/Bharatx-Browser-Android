Privacy Policy

BharatX Browser for Android, developed by Nitaistudio. Policy version: 31 October 2026.

Data on your device

History, bookmarks, cookies, site permissions, preferences, saved logins and download records may be stored on your device as you browse. Use the browser controls to manage or delete this information. Private browsing limits the normal history saved by the app; it does not make network activity anonymous.

Websites, search and services

Opening a website sends requests to that website and the network services needed to load it. Searches and enabled search suggestions are sent to the selected search provider. Website operators and your network provider may process information such as your IP address. Optional sign-in, sync, security checks, translation, DNS and add-on features may connect to their service providers and follow those providers' policies.

Optional BharatX VPN

VPN is off until you choose a server, accept the separate VPN disclosure and grant Android VPN permission. Only BharatX Browser traffic is routed through the selected VPN server; other apps are excluded. The VPN encrypts the connection between your device and that server. Its operator receives your source IP address and can observe connection destinations, DNS requests and traffic not separately protected by HTTPS. Server operators have their own privacy practices. No claim is made that these imported servers keep no logs.

The VPN may continue while the browser is in the background. Disconnect in the VPN screen, its notification or Android VPN settings. The SDK attempts to reconnect an interrupted session up to five times. During a drop, browsing may use the normal network; this feature does not promise a kill switch. This integration does not send browsing logs to Nitaistudio. Traffic totals and connection state are shown locally. Diagnostic logging is disabled by this app.

When a server requires provider login, the details you enter are used by the VPN SDK to authenticate to that server and reconnect the current session. BharatX does not save them in preferences or saved screen state. The SDK manages its own session files in private app storage. Premium access in this app is unlocked by a completed video or an active monthly subscription. Separate provider credentials are needed only when the chosen profile requires them. The supplied free server configurations include shared client certificates and keys; they are not personal account credentials.

Advertising choices

Optional Appnext ads can process device/app identifiers, installed-app information, ad activity and location, subject to permissions. Enable them through Ad choices; decline to keep the SDK inactive, or revoke to stop new ad requests. This integration does not provide browsing history or VPN traffic to Appnext. Appnext Privacy Policy.

Monthly subscription and rewards

Google Play handles monthly payments and subscription records. BharatX checks current purchases to remove ads; it does not receive payment-card details. Pending payments do not unlock access. Price and currency are supplied by Google Play. Manage renewal/cancellation in Google Play. A completed rewarded video unlocks one Premium session locally; failed attempts before its first successful connection retain that credit. Reward credit is held in memory and can be lost if the app process ends. Subscription benefits are restored from Google Play, not a local paid flag.

Permissions

Camera, microphone, location, notifications, media access and other permissions support browser or website features. Grant only permissions you want to use; review and revoke them in Android Settings. Downloads you keep remain on your device until you remove them.

Extensions

Extensions can access data allowed by the permissions you grant. Review each extension's permissions and privacy policy. You can disable or remove extensions in the browser.

Diagnostics

This distribution sets the application's telemetry and automatic crash-reporting build switches to off. This does not prevent websites or separately enabled third-party services from making network requests.

Support and changes

If you email support, your email provider and the developer receive the message and the information you choose to include. Do not send passwords or sensitive browsing data. An updated policy may accompany a later app release.
